# Code of Conduct

We want Bitstraining to be a place where students, researchers and engineers can ask questions and
contribute without being made to feel small.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient; assume good faith; give specific, kind feedback; credit other
people's work; keep discussion about the work, not the person.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or
attention, sustained disruption, or publishing others' private information.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may restrict participation for
behavior that violates this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the repository;
reports are handled confidentially and in good faith.

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any chat the project runs.
